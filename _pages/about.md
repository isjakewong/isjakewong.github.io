I am a second-year Ph.D. student of Computer Science at Cornell University and a Cornell [Presidential Life Science](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/financial-support/fellowships/presidential-life-science-fellowship/) Fellow, advised by Prof. [Carla P. Gomes](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/gomes/). I've also worked closely with Profs. [Volodymyr Kuleshov](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~kuleshov/), [Chris De Sa](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~cdesa/), and [Fei Wang](https://wcm-wanglab.github.io). Previously, I studied Biomedical Engineering at Johns Hopkins University and Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University. My current research interest lies in artificial intelligence (AI) with its applications in natural science, life science, and sustainability. Specifically, my research spans machine learning on graphs and geometry, probabilistic inference and generative modeling, self-supervised learning, constrained optimization, and AI for scientific discovery.

Please drop me an email if you'd like to chat! 😃
